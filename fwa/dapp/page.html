01

Choose your side.

Two ways to use FWA. Both carry risk.

PLAY
↯

Acquire one random position.

Pay the current acquisition price. The protocol assigns one eligible position from the pool.

  • Light backing is selected more often
  • Heavy backing is selected less often
  • Chainlink VRF supplies the randomness
DEPOSIT
＋

Pair an NFT with ETH backing.

Backing funds the standing bid, sets selection weight and keeps the position active in the pool.

  • Active positions share acquisition fees
  • More backing usually means a longer stay
  • Longer expected duration is not safety

03

What actually matters.

Read this before looking at the biggest NFT in the pool.

01

Backing is committed capital.

It funds the standing bid and controls selection weight. It is not an independent NFT valuation.

02

Big backing means low odds.

The highest-backed positions are generally the rarest outcomes.

03

The pool keeps moving.

Deposits, allocations, relists and withdrawals can change price and probabilities.

04

Past outcomes do not make a target due.

Every request uses the eligible pool state assigned to it and settles in order.

04

How players approach FWA.

Practical habits — not a way to control VRF.

READ THE WHOLE POOL

A headline asset says little on its own. Lightly backed positions dominate typical outcomes.

CHOOSE YOUR EXIT FIRST

Know when you would keep the NFT, take ETH or take FWA before the reveal.

SET A HARD BUDGET

Previous losses do not improve the next result. Do not chase a target.

KEEP WHAT YOU WANT

Compare the NFT with the standing bid. Rarity alone does not create demand.

USE NO-REGRET BACKING

Depositors should choose a level where an early selection and a longer stay are both acceptable.

TIME, NOT IMMUNITY

More backing buys expected duration. Any active position can still be selected early.

05 · RISKS

Know what can go wrong.

FWA is experimental. Check the current app and parameters before interacting.

LIKELY OUTCOME

You are far more likely to receive a lightly backed position.

DOWNSIDE

The selected position can have lower backing than your acquisition cost. The bid pays only its configured share.

MARKET RISK

Backing does not guarantee resale demand, liquidity or profit.

PROTOCOL RISK

Deposits can leave early, withdrawals may wait for earlier requests, and parameters or token value can change.

01

Back first.
Launch second.

FWAIR separates NFT supply from ETH backing, then joins both together inside normal FWA positions.

ARTISTNFTs

The artist escrows the complete approved ERC-721 range. No backing ETH is required from the artist.

SUPPORTERSETH

Supporters back sequential NFTs at one fixed backing amount. They cannot choose a specific token ID.

FULL BACKINGLIVE

The final backing activates and whitelists the collection, then permissionless calls submit NFTs into FWA.

CLAIMSPULL

ETH, FWA rewards and NFT entitlements accrue onchain first and are claimed by the recorded beneficiary.

Important: “Complete” means every campaign NFT has been submitted to FWA. It does not mean every listing has already been purchased or settled.
